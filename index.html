What it does

🌱 Reconnect

It quietly finds people you haven't spoken to in a while — a balanced mix of recent and long‑lost — and writes each one a short, warm message picking up a real thread.

🧠 Remember

Every contact gets a living profile: who they are, their language, the way you write to them, your shared history, and a little note so you always know who's who.

💬 Invite personally

Pick a group, say what you want — "invite everyone to Sunday" — and it drafts an individual 1:1 for each member. Not a broadcast. It even reaches friends whose number WhatsApp hides, through your existing chat with them.

How it works

1

Link WhatsApp — scan a QR once, the same way as WhatsApp Web. Your messages stay on your computer; there's no server.

2

It learns your voice — from your own past messages, so drafts sound like you (your greetings, your length, your emoji) and in each person's language.

3

You review — read each draft, edit anything, approve the good ones, skip the rest. You're always in control.

4

It sends, gently — approved messages go out one at a time with natural pauses, a daily limit, and an extra‑strict limit on brand‑new contacts. No spam, no blasting.

Bring your old chats

WhaGrOU sounds like you because it reads how you've actually written to each person — so richer history means better drafts. Export a chat from WhatsApp (Export Chat → Without Media) and load the .txt in Settings → Import old data. It's read on your Mac and never uploaded. How to export & import →

Install on Mac

1. Download WhaGrOU-2.1.1-arm64-mac.zip and unzip it. Drag WhaGrOU into your Applications folder.

2. The first time, macOS may say it's from an unidentified developer. Right‑click the app → Open → Open. (You only do this once.)

If it still won't open, open Terminal and run: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/WhaGrOU.app

Install on Windows

1. Download WhaGrOU-2.1.1-setup.exe and run it. It installs just for you — no admin password needed.

2. Windows may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” screen, because the app isn't code‑signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway. You only do this once.

Prefer not to install? Grab the portable .zip from the releases page, unzip it anywhere, and run WhaGrOU.exe.

Sending safely

Nothing sends on its own — you set the pace by what you approve, so for a bigger push you approve in waves and watch how the first day lands. Two limits live in Settings: a daily cap, and a separate, much stricter cap for first‑ever contacts (people you've never messaged — the real risk). What matters most isn't the number, it's that people welcome the message: keep it to your own community, in your own voice, and watch for any blocks. More on safe sending →

Your privacy

WhaGrOU runs entirely on your own computer. Your contacts, chats, and drafts live in a local file on your Mac — there is no WhaGrOU server and nothing is uploaded. Drafting uses your own AI key. You decide what gets sent, and to whom.
